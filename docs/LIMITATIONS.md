# Release limitations

- The initial 20 ns ADC-input budget failed routed timing by 3.319 ns at a 100 ns clock. Unchanged geometry was requalified using a 15 ns ADC-code/valid input budget, retaining 20 ns for other inputs and outputs. Worst setup slack is +1.681 ns and hold slack +0.107 ns across the available corners. This conditional interface constraint is not a measured maximum frequency.
- Magic DRC, KLayout DRC and Netgen LVS passed on the original routed geometry. The timing recheck did not alter that geometry. Slew and capacitance warnings remain in the reports. These are installed flow checks, not foundry-qualified signoff. The core has no pads, ESD or package.
- Numerical geometry/material properties are assumed. There are no measured CDTA data, experimental sensors, fabricated silicon, ppm/selectivity results, safety certification or admissions claims.
- Primary geometry is a resolved 2-D thin-film FVM. Through-thickness gradients, interface thermal resistance and independent 3-D validation are absent. The transient uses an electrically lagged source with implicit thermal integration; the recorded time-step refinement quantifies the split-solver error.
- The held-out RTL-in-loop nominal hold RMSE is 2.34 C over the defined window. It misses the initial <=2 C target. No favourable window was substituted after testing. A Python campaign and a separate explicitly centered ideal front end are not identical experimental configurations.
- RTL-in-loop testing accelerates CONTROL_DIV to 32 while advancing the plant at the declared 1 ms sample time. PWM/endpoints are separately tested, but the accelerated run is not a clock-accurate simulation of every physical PWM period.
- Off-state cooldown samples are invalid. The core is disabled during cooldown; stale may latch and PWM remains off. A small measurement probe during commanded-zero powered phases is included in heater energy. Ideal front-end amplifier energy remains unmodelled.
- Six actual-RTL training candidates used a separate 50-to-60 C schedule; the 55-to-65 C schedule was held out. The final nominal trace has no powered-phase fault. The broader Python campaign publishes settling failures, paired traces and seeds; feedforward energy improvement is not assumed.
- Only the primary arithmetic core is routed. Width comparisons in Python are numeric/range studies, not evidence of silicon-area improvement. Alternative multiplier architectures, a geometry/surrogate Pareto sweep, the separate transistor OTA macro and gate-level/formal equivalence remain PFE work.
- A full independent clean-image physical rerun was not performed. Installed-image tests and the earlier routed core were reused with source/config hashes. Historical failed outputs are retained as failed evidence, not promoted to successful results.
