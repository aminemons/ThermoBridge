## Calibration and tracking

The v3 protocol freezes 12 gain/calibration candidates across three training schedules. Its 52 actual RTL runs select Kp 1638/16384, Ki 32/16384 per sample and calibration at 45/70 C. Five of six fresh cases miss the 2 C hold-RMSE target at the original 0.15 V supply.

An ideal no-preheating RC reference identifies heating-rate sensitivity. The reference fixes the pre-step temperature and assumes perfect readout. Anticipatory trajectories need their own analysis.

The prospective v4 protocol fixes the controller selected by v3 and compares external heater supplies of 0.15 and 0.18 V. Its 19 actual RTL runs include six further fresh pairs and two regression pairs. At 0.18 V, the fresh cases span 1.675-1.777 C hold RMSE with at most 1.297 C hold overshoot. All powered-phase fault counts are zero. Available peak heater power rises 44%.

## Spatial operating-point check

A separate 25 ms full-duty pulse and cooldown at 0.18 V uses the primary spatial geometry and original compact parameters. The finer 0.25 ms run has 0.128 C RMS and 0.226 C maximum absolute model error. Time-step refinement changes the trajectory by 0.324%. The spatial temperature peak is 99.173 C, within the declared material-model range.
