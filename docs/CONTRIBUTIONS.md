# Authors and Contributions

**Authors and maintainers:** [Amine Allab](https://github.com/aminemons) and [Ishaq Kolla](https://github.com/Ishaq-ML).

ThermoBridge is a collaborative engineering research project. The authors share responsibility for the project's technical development, verification and scientific reporting, covering electrothermal modelling, numerical experiments, fixed-point feedback control, RTL validation and the open-source digital implementation flow.

## Repository contributions

Development is documented by the source code, version history, test outputs, configuration files and reproducibility records. Ishaq Kolla contributed the contributor quick-start guide in commit `6e23643`, merged through [pull request #1](https://github.com/aminemons/ThermoBridge/pull/1) on 28 October 2026. This public change is one recorded contribution, not an exhaustive account of the collaboration.

The 30 October revision adds the integral quantization study, paired RTL evaluation, geometry screen, refined candidates and updated technical article. [Revision review](REVIEW_20261030.md) and [reproduction instructions](REPRODUCE.md) describe the available evidence and its limits.

## Scientific integrity and third-party resources

Numerical findings and physical-design checks are reported within their documented assumptions. Independent validation and hardware measurements remain future work. Third-party PDKs, example designs and software retain their respective licenses and attributions.
